# Example: Reconciliation loops beat one-off plans

> [!example] Example note
> Seeded to show the conventions. Safe to delete or archive once you have real notes.

A plan is only correct on the day it is written. What keeps a goal alive is a loop that regularly compares the desired state with the actual state and makes a small correction, the way Terraform or a Kubernetes controller does.

## Why it matters
It is the reason the weekly review exists: the review is the diff, and next week's plan is the apply.
